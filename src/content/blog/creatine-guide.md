Creatine spent thirty years as a bodybuilding product. Now it is one of the fastest-growing supplements in the world, bought by women in their forties, people worried about brain fog, and adults looking after aging parents. The molecule has not changed; the understanding of who it helps has. Here is a clear, no-hype guide to what creatine actually does and how to use it.

<div class="key-takeaway"><strong>The short answer</strong>Creatine helps muscles produce energy for short, intense effort, improving strength and training performance. Creatine monohydrate is the most studied form; 3 to 5 g a day, every day, is the standard dose. It is one of the best-supported, safest supplements for healthy adults. Consistency matters more than timing.</div>

## What creatine is and what it does

Creatine is a compound your body already makes and stores in muscle, where it helps regenerate energy during short bursts of intense effort, think a heavy set, a sprint, or a jump. You also get small amounts from meat and fish. Supplementing raises how much your muscles store, so you can produce a little more force and do a little more work before fatiguing.

That extra work, repeated over weeks and months, is what drives the main benefit: **more strength, power and training volume**, which support building and preserving muscle. Newer research is also investigating creatine's role in **healthy aging** (preserving muscle and strength later in life) and even **cognitive function**, though those areas are less settled than the performance evidence.

## The proven benefits

The strongest, most consistent evidence is for physical performance:

- **Strength and power**: more force in short, intense efforts
- **Training capacity**: a little more volume per session, which adds up over time
- **Muscle**: better results from resistance training, and help preserving muscle during weight loss or aging

Because preserving muscle is central to [weight maintenance](/blog/life-after-glp-1/) and healthy aging, creatine pairs naturally with a [high-protein diet](/food-lists/high-protein-foods/) and resistance training. Protein supplies the building material; creatine helps you train hard enough to use it.

<div class="highlight"><p><strong>Why it matters:</strong> creatine is one of very few supplements where the performance evidence is genuinely strong and consistent. Most supplements promise far more than they deliver; creatine is the rare one that does roughly what the research says.</p></div>

## How to take it

The practical guidance is refreshingly simple:

- **Form**: creatine monohydrate. It is the most researched, most effective and cheapest form. The fancier, pricier versions do not outperform it.
- **Dose**: 3 to 5 g per day, every day, including rest days.
- **Loading (optional)**: around 20 g a day split into four doses for 5 to 7 days saturates muscles faster. Skipping it is fine; steady daily dosing reaches the same place in a few weeks.
- **Timing**: far less important than consistency. Any time of day works; after training with a meal is a reasonable default.

The single biggest factor in whether creatine works is **taking it consistently**, because it works by building up stored levels over time, not by an immediate hit.

## The common myths

Three worries come up again and again, and the evidence is reassuring on all three. **Hair loss** traces to one small, unreplicated study and is not supported by the wider research. **Weight gain** in the first weeks is water held in the muscle, not fat. And creatine is **not just for men or bodybuilders**, which is exactly why interest among women and older adults is rising so fast; see our guide to [creatine for women](/blog/creatine-for-women/).

## The bottom line

Creatine is a rare supplement that lives up to its evidence: **creatine monohydrate, 3 to 5 g daily, taken consistently**, reliably supports strength, training and muscle, with a strong safety record for healthy adults. It is not a magic powder and it does not replace good training or [enough protein](/calculators/protein/), but as an add-on it is one of the most proven options available.
